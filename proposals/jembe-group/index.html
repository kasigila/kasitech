Letter from the Founder

Dear Jembe Leadership,

I read the 2026 corporate profile before writing this. The work is serious: operating solar in Shinyanga and Bukombe, grid substations, a national water-backbone file, a medical scheme covering thousands of lives, a gold-processing analysis in Tarime. That is not a generic conglomerate slide.

A PDF cannot do what a website must do for a group like this. A sponsor, DFI, ministry, or insurer arriving cold needs to understand the five sectors, see the energy assets as operating facts, and reach a desk that routes by counterpart and sector — in the first minute.

05 · Digital Position
Current Digital Position

The profile is institutional. The public web is not yet.

This is not a teardown of a live site. Jembe supplied a Corporate Profile 2026. The gap is between that file and what a counterparty can use on the open web.

What already exists
  • A 2026 corporate profile with sector map, energy assets, network entities, and selected engagements
  • Operating facts that can be tabulated: 50 MW Shinyanga, 5 MW Bukombe, TANESCO off-take, 132/33 kV substations
  • A delivery story through BQ Contractors Ltd (ISO 9001:2015) that is rare for an advisory group
What a PDF cannot do
  • A ministry or DFI cannot search sectors, assets, and offices from a phone in a corridor
  • A generic inbox does not route sponsor vs lender vs scheme vs EPC
  • Five desks (Mauritius and East Africa) need a ledger, not a single footer line
Facts pending confirmation
  • Addresses and telephone numbers as printed in the profile — confirm before a live domain
  • Domain www.jembegroup.com as stated in the profile — confirm ownership and DNS
  • Public emails (info@, country desks) — confirm which should publish
Integrity rule
  • No invented MW, lives covered, or mandate outcomes beyond the profile
  • Photography is licensed atmosphere plus the group mark — not claimed as Jembe site photography
  • The preview is labelled as a proposed website until Jembe accepts and facts are confirmed
Assessment summary

Jembe already has the substance. The website’s job is to hold that substance in a form a counterparty can use: sector wall, asset tables, tombstones, office ledger, mandate desk. The preview does that work. Launch waits on confirmation, not on a new concept.
