06 · Care Plan
Professional Care · CARE-PRO

What monthly care covers after launch.

CARE-PRO at TSh 150,000 / month keeps the site healthy and current within a professional care tier. Exact response hours and monthly update allowance are confirmed in the care onboarding note after launch (and may be refined in writing without changing the catalog fee unless scope changes).

Included (typical CARE-PRO)

Routine updates and light content changes within the care allowance · monitoring for obvious breakage · plugin/platform maintenance as applicable · monthly check-in rhythm · guidance on small improvements · coordination on review and local profile hygiene started under this build.

Not included in CARE-PRO

Full redesign or rebuild · launching a new vertical as a major new site section · social media management · paid ads · custom development beyond the care allowance · emergency work outside agreed response windows (quoted separately or via CARE-PRI upgrade).

StartAt website launch (or written alternative start date)
BillingMonthly in advance
Upgrade pathPriority Care (CARE-PRI) at TSh 300,000 / month replaces CARE-PRO if Credo chooses it in writing. It is not stacked on top of CARE-PRO.
CancellationEither party may end CARE-PRO with 30 days’ written notice. Prepaid unused care for a fully unused month after the notice period ends may be discussed in good faith; milestone website fees already earned remain due.
SLA confirmation

Detailed response times and the monthly content/update allowance will be written into the care onboarding note within 10 business days of launch. Until that note is issued, KasiTech will provide commercially reasonable professional care consistent with CARE-PRO.

07 · Changes · IP · Confidentiality
Operating Rules

Changes, ownership, and confidentiality.

Change control

Work outside the Scope page (including optional catalog add-ons, new vertical microsites, or material redesign after design approval) requires a written change order or updated quotation stating fee and impact on timeline. KasiTech will not invent scope. Credo will not assume free extras.

Intellectual property

Credo materialsLogos, photos, trademarks, and copy Credo supplies remain Credo’s. Credo grants KasiTech a licence to use them to perform this Agreement.
DeliverablesOn full payment of the one-time fees, Credo owns the final website deliverables created specifically for Credo under this Agreement (pages, Credo-specific design, Credo copy as delivered).
KasiTech toolsKasiTech retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, frameworks, internal processes, and generic components. Credo receives a licence to use them as embodied in the delivered site.
PortfolioUnless Credo objects in writing, KasiTech may show the project (name, screenshots, high-level outcomes) in its portfolio and case materials after launch.

Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other party’s non-public business information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or already public through no fault of the receiving party. This duty continues for 3 years after the Agreement ends, and longer for trade secrets as long as they remain secret.

No invented claims

KasiTech will not invent Credo projects, revenue, headcount, or performance claims. Proof content appears when Credo supplies it.
