## Digital observations (public-facing only)

These are **observations of publicly visible digital presence**, not confidential audits:

1. **Product breadth vs. digital clarity:** The public site presents a wide portfolio (solar, utilities, EV, drones, telecom). Digital storytelling and navigation do not yet match that portfolio depth with equal clarity.
2. **Trust signals:** Placeholder About content and inconsistent contact details weaken executive / partner confidence online.
3. **Lead capture:** Quote/order CTAs exist; form service taxonomy appears misaligned with Credo’s stated businesses.
4. **Content engine:** Limited evidence of a sustained news/thought-leadership rhythm matching a multi-vertical energy & technology group.
5. **Opportunity:** A coherent digital system can position Credo as the credible African energy & smart-infrastructure partner it aspires to be — for customers, distributors, and institutional counterparts.

## Competitive / category context (general, not named competitors)

Energy and smart-infrastructure buyers typically evaluate:
- Credibility and proof (projects, partners, certifications — *to be confirmed with Credo*)
- Product clarity and specification access
- Responsiveness of enquiry channels
- Professionalism of digital and social presence relative to regional peers
