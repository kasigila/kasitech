Who we are

A group built for complex markets.

Mauritius-incorporated. East Africa focused.

Jembe Group LLC is a multi-sector consulting and investment group. We originate, structure, and deliver mandates by bringing sector specialists, structuring discipline, and an established network into a single accountable point of delivery.

Our work covers complex projects: power plants, national infrastructure, mining ventures, agro-industrial estates, and risk-transfer programmes that need to be bankable before they are built.

The Group

A group built for complex markets.

Cross-border structuring, bankable feasibility, blended-finance structures, commodity off-take, and risk-transfer programmes, delivered as one coordinated engagement.

Incorporation

Mauritius head office. East African desks.

The group is Mauritius-incorporated, with regional offices in Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Kampala, and Kigali. Work concentrates on Tanzania and the wider East African market, with structuring discipline that is written for lenders, DFIs, and sponsors.

We originate and structure mandates, then bring in the legal, insurance, technical, and finance specialists needed to take them from assessment to execution.
