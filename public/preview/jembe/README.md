# Jembe Group LLC - proposed corporate website

**Prepared by:** KasiTech  
**Prepared for:** Jembe Group LLC  
**Live domain:** www.jembegroupllc.com  
**Source:** Corporate Profile 2026 (client PDF)  
**Live preview:** `/preview/jembe/`

A full institutional website, not a KasiTech marketing page. Brand, copy, offices, assets, entities, and selected engagements are taken from the client profile. Statistics that are not in the PDF are not invented.

## Design

- Navy `#02122C`, champagne gold `#C9A56A`, azure wing mark `#0082FC` (logo only - not used as a SaaS accent colour)
- Diamond hairline rules from the profile cover
- Five-column photographic sector wall (profile page 2)
- Investment-memo asset tables for Shinyanga / Bukombe / substations
- Deal tombstones for track record
- Office ledger for five desks
- Mandate desk (segmented counterpart × sector) instead of a generic contact form

## Mandate form

Submissions post to **info@jembegroupllc.com** via [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co). The first live submission may require confirming the inbox once (FormSubmit activation email). Until then, the on-page error note points visitors to email the desk directly.

## Integrity

Company-specific statements are grounded in the supplied Corporate Profile 2026. Confirm Mauritius / East Africa addresses and numbers with the client before launch on www.jembegroupllc.com.
