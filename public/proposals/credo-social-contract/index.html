Purpose

Credo engages KasiTech to plan, produce, publish, and report on professional social media for Credo under Social Pro (SOC-PRO), as described in Proposal KT-CEG-SOC-2026-001. This Agreement states the binding commercial and operating terms for that engagement.

If there is a conflict between this Agreement and the Proposal, this Agreement controls for legal and commercial terms. The Proposal remains the descriptive scope reference (voice, pillars, calendar shape).

Separate engagements

Website design, build, SEO, and website care are not included. If Credo also signs a website agreement, that work is governed by KT-CEG-WEB-AGR-2026-001 (or its successor). Neither agreement requires acceptance of the other.

05 · Operating Terms
How We Work

Calendar, approvals, and kickoff needs.

Operating rhythm

CalendarKasiTech issues a monthly content calendar for Credo review before production lock.
ApprovalsCredo names one approver. Target: feedback within 2 business days. Silence after a written reminder may be treated as approval for scheduled items that are factually based on Credo-supplied material.
RevisionsReasonable revisions within the approved calendar are included. Material new concepts after lock may move to the next cycle or be quoted as extra.
Community hoursBusiness hours East Africa Time, Monday to Friday, unless otherwise agreed in writing.
VoiceInstitutional, clear, and credible. Not meme-driven. Aligned to the Brand Voice direction in Proposal KT-CEG-SOC-2026-001.

Kickoff needs from Credo

Brand assets
Logo files, colours, typography, guidelines, or written interim direction.

Channel access
Instagram and Facebook admin / Business Manager roles. Confirm ownership of handles (including @credoenergygroup_ltd if used).

Point of contact
One Credo approver for calendar and creative turnaround.

Source material
Product facts, approved photography, project claims Credo is willing to publish.

Dependency note

If assets or access are late, publishing volume for that month may be reduced without reducing the fee for time already reserved, or the start date may shift by written agreement. KasiTech will flag gaps honestly rather than invent content.

06 · IP · Confidentiality · Conduct
Ownership & Conduct

Who owns what, and how we protect Credo.

Credo materialsBrand assets, photos, trademarks, and claims Credo supplies remain Credo’s. Credo grants KasiTech a licence to use them to perform this Agreement.
Creative deliverablesOn payment for the relevant month, Credo may use the creatives produced for that month on its channels. KasiTech may keep working files and reuse generic techniques and templates.
Stock / toolsLicensed stock, fonts, or tools remain under their own licences. KasiTech will not knowingly use assets Credo cannot publish.
PortfolioUnless Credo objects in writing, KasiTech may show anonymised or named Credo social work in its portfolio after posts are public.
ConfidentialityNon-public business information stays confidential and is used only to perform this Agreement, except where disclosure is required by law or already public through no fault of the receiving party. Continues 3 years after the Agreement ends.
No invented claimsKasiTech will not invent Credo projects, performance metrics, or endorsements. Missing facts are flagged, not fabricated.
Platform rulesBoth parties will respect Meta and other platform terms. Credo remains the account owner.
Comments and crises

Routine community replies are included in business hours. Issues that look like a public crisis, legal dispute, or major PR event should be escalated to Credo’s approver immediately. Extended crisis support may be quoted separately.

07 · Term & General
Term · Cancellation · General

How the engagement runs, and how it ends.

TermMonth-to-month starting on the Effective Date (or agreed start date), renewing automatically each month.
CancellationEither party may cancel with 30 days’ written notice. Fees already due for the notice period remain payable. Work in the final month is delivered on a reasonable wind-down basis.
SuspensionKasiTech may pause publishing if invoices are unpaid after reminder, or if Credo withdraws channel access.
LimitationExcept for unpaid fees, confidentiality breaches, or wilful misconduct, each party’s total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid to KasiTech in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, including platform algorithm changes.
StatusKasiTech is an independent contractor, not Credo’s employee or partner.
SubcontractorsKasiTech may use vetted subcontractors (for example design or editing support) under its supervision and remains responsible for their work.
NoticesEmail to the contacts on the Signature page (or updated in writing) is sufficient written notice.
Governing lawLaws of the United Republic of Tanzania. Courts / competent forums in Dar es Salaam, unless the parties agree in writing to mediation first.
Entire agreementThis Agreement plus any signed change orders is the full agreement for social media management. The Proposal is an attached descriptive reference.
Optional Meta setup

If Credo ticks ADS-META-S on the Signature page, KasiTech will perform one-time Meta Ads Setup for TSh 200,000. Ongoing ads management and media spend are not included unless a separate written scope is added later.
