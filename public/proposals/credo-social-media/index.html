02 · Letter
Letter from the Founder

Why social, and why now.

Dear Credo Leadership,

Credo Energy Group presents itself publicly as a Tanzanian conglomerate advancing sustainable development, innovation, and industrial transformation across Africa. Your portfolio — solar cooling, smart utilities, renewables, EV, drones, and digital/telecom — is the kind of breadth that institutional buyers, distributors, and partners notice.

What they also notice is whether your digital voice matches that seriousness. Public trust gaps on credogroupllc.com (placeholder About content, inconsistent contact details, misaligned enquiry taxonomy) mean social channels cannot afford to sound casual or meme-driven. Social must reinforce institutional credibility while the website engagement proceeds on its own track.

This proposal covers social media management only. A separate website proposal — KT-CEG-WEB-2026-001 — addresses the corporate site. The two engagements complement each other; neither depends on accepting the other.

We recommend Social Pro (SOC-PRO) from the KasiTech price book: a professional corporate cadence for Instagram and Facebook, with LinkedIn as a later option once the visual and voice system is stable.

I look forward to building Credo’s public voice with the same discipline you bring to infrastructure.

03 · Brand Voice
Credo Brand Voice for Social

Confident. Technical. Africa-forward. Never casual for effect.

Social for an energy and technology conglomerate should sound like a boardroom briefing, not a lifestyle brand. Tone will be refined with Credo brand assets once supplied.

Register Institutional Clear, precise language. Prefer outcomes and capability over hype.
Visual Infrastructure-led Real products, sites, and systems. Atmosphere with purpose — not stock cliché.
Audience B2B & partners Distributors, commercial buyers, utilities, institutional counterparts — then public awareness.
We will
  • Lead with technology, projects, and industry perspective
  • Use captions that explain value in plain executive English
  • Keep hashtags sparse, relevant, and market-aware
  • Credit field work and people with dignity
  • Align visuals to Credo brand once guidelines are shared
  • Escalate sensitive topics (policy, claims, partners) for Credo approval
We will not
  • Run meme formats, slang trends, or viral bait
  • Invent project outcomes or certifications
  • Overpromise delivery timelines or product performance
  • Post unverified competitor comparisons
  • Use placeholder team names or stock “success” stories as Credo fact
  • Treat social as a substitute for a credible website
Voice sample (illustrative)

“Across solar cooling, smart metering, and renewable infrastructure, Credo designs systems that communities and operators can measure. This week: how prepaid utility metering reduces commercial loss — and what operators should ask before they buy.”

Reach · Community
Facebook

Broader distribution for announcements, shared feed creative, and community conversation during business hours (EAT).

05 · Content Pillars
Content Pillars

Four pillars. One conglomerate story.

Every post maps to a pillar. Mix shifts month to month with Credo priorities and approved assets — never random posting.

Pillar 01
Technology

Products and capabilities across the Credo portfolio: smart solar AC, utility metering, renewables, EV, drones, digital/telecom. Explain what it is, who it serves, and why it matters — without inventing specs Credo has not approved.

Pillar 02
Projects

Field proof: installs, deployments, and outcomes using Credo-supplied photography and facts. Where project details are pending confirmation, we hold or frame content as capability narrative — never fabricated case studies.

Pillar 03
Industry POV

Energy transition, smart utilities, and infrastructure perspective for Tanzania and Africa. Positions Credo as a thinking operator — measured, useful, and executive-grade — not a news aggregator.

Pillar 04
People

Team, partners, and customer success told with dignity. Spotlights and culture moments that humanise Credo without drifting into lifestyle fluff. Names and quotes only with Credo approval.

08 · Workflow
Operating Workflow

Brief → Create → Approve → Publish → Report.

A clear monthly loop so Credo stays in control of messaging while KasiTech runs production and community execution.

01
Brief

Monthly strategy call or written brief. Priorities, launches, constraints, and asset availability from Credo.

02
Create

Content calendar, copy, design, and short-form edits aligned to Credo brand (pending brand assets).

03
Approve

Credo reviews calendar and creative. Feedback consolidated in one round where possible for cadence.

04
Publish

Scheduling across Instagram and Facebook. Stories and reels timed to the approved plan. Community replies in EAT business hours.

05
Report

Monthly report: reach, engagement, top content, learnings, and recommendations for the next cycle.

Credo responsibilities

Approve messaging and creative · Supply brand assets and project facts · Confirm channel access · Flag sensitive topics early · Designate a single point of contact for turnaround.

KasiTech responsibilities

Monthly strategy · Production within SOC-PRO scope · Scheduling · Community management (business hours EAT) · Reporting · Honest flagging when facts or assets are missing.
