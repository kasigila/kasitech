# Demo Studio image strategy

Assets under `public/demo/{amani,jiko,tembea,nuru}/` are project-owned copies of
[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) photographs (free to use commercially;
no watermarks). Sourced for fictional Tanzanian demo businesses in Phase 3.1.

They are served locally (not hotlinked) so production previews never depend on
third-party CDN availability.
