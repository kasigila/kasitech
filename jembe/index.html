The Group

A group built for complex markets.

Cross-border structuring, bankable feasibility, blended-finance structures, commodity off-take, and risk-transfer programmes — assembled as one delivery.

Incorporation

Mauritius head office. East African desks.

The group is Mauritius-incorporated, with regional offices in Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Kampala, and Kigali. Work concentrates on Tanzania and the wider East African market, with structuring discipline that is written for lenders, DFIs, and sponsors.

We do not sell a catalogue of products. We originate and structure mandates, then assemble the legal, insurance, technical, and finance specialists required to take them from assessment to execution.
